About Our Church

A place to build godly relationships, grow in faith, and stand firm in God’s Word.

Our Mission

To preach Christ, make disciples, grow believers in the Word of God, and serve our community in love.

Our Vision

To become a spiritually mature, united, and Christ-centered church that transforms lives and communities through the Gospel.

Our Core Foundation Values

“Rooted and built up in him, strengthened in the faith as you were taught, and overflowing with thankfulness.”
— Colossians 2:7
  • Biblical Truth: Unwavering commitment to God's inspired Word.
  • Love & Compassion: Demonstrating Christ’s love to all people.
  • Prayer & Faith: Continual reliance on prayer in every season.
  • Unity & Fellowship: Standing together as one family in Christ.
  • Holiness & Integrity: Living honorably in light of the Gospel.
  • Servant Leadership: Leading through humility and dedicated service.
  • Evangelism & Discipleship: Reaching the lost and equipping believers.
  • Stewardship & Generosity: Joyfully sharing our time, talents, and resources.

Our Church Family & Ministries

Children & Sunday School Ministry

Nurturing the next generation with God's love and foundational Bible teachings. We are committed to caring for our Sunday school children and supporting orphans in our community with love, guidance, and spiritual growth.

Our Pastoral Leadership

Guided by faith and dedicated service, our Senior Pastor and family lead with love, prayer, and devotion to shepherd the congregation and serve the community faithfully.

Youth Ministry

Empowering young adults to grow deep in faith, discover their purpose, and build strong, Christ-centered friendships through fellowship, worship, and active community outreach.

Choir & Church Ministers

Leading the congregation in vibrant worship and praise. Our dedicated choir and church ministers serve untiringly to elevate every worship experience and glorify God.
